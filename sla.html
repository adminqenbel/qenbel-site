1. Who this covers

This SLA applies to clients who have a written agreement with QenBel, and only to the work and systems that agreement covers. Visitors without an agreement can still write to us through the support page or by email, and we will reply, but the response targets below do not apply to them.

2. How to raise a request

All support is by email. We do not offer calls, meetings or chat sign-off.

  • Use the support page, which prepares the email for you, or write to admin.qenbel@gmail.com.
  • Include your company and project or agreement name, a one line summary, the priority you suggest (section 3), what you saw and when it started.
  • Do not include passwords, API keys, secrets, customer data or source code. If we need anything like that, we will tell you how to share it safely.

The time a request counts from is the time it reaches our inbox, not the time it was written.

3. Priority levels and response targets

You suggest a priority when you write. We confirm it, or change it with a short reason, in our first reply.

PriorityWhat it meansFirst reply targetUpdates
1 Urgent A live system that QenBel built, maintains or secures is down or unusable, or a security incident affects a live system, and there is no workaround. [X hours] Every [X hours] until resolved or a plan is agreed
2 High An important function is broken or badly degraded, but the system is still usable or there is a workaround. [X hours] Every [X working days]
3 Normal A fault or question that does not stop people doing their work. [X working days] When there is progress
4 Low A general question, a small improvement or a request for information. [X working days] When there is progress

Response targets are counted within the support hours in section 4.

5. What a response means

A first reply means a person at QenBel has read your request, confirmed the priority and told you what happens next. It is not a fix. How long a fix takes depends on the problem, and [we do not set fix-time targets, or: fix targets are as follows, to confirm].

6. Changes to your systems

We work only with your written authorisation. We never change a production system without a verified backup, testing and your release approval. This applies to urgent requests too: we may ask for your approval by email before a fix goes live, and the time you take to approve is not counted against our targets.

7. What we need from you

  • Name the people who may raise requests and approve changes for you, and tell us when that changes.
  • Give us the access and information we ask for, through the safe channel we agree.
  • Answer our questions promptly. Time spent waiting for you is not counted against our targets.
  • Make sure we have written authorisation for any security testing, and that you own or may authorise the systems involved.

8. What is outside this SLA

  • Work or systems your written agreement with us does not cover.
  • Faults caused by changes made by someone other than QenBel, or by software or services run by other companies, such as hosting, cloud, payment or email providers.
  • Requests made without the authorisation described in section 7.
  • New features or projects. These are quoted and agreed separately, by email, in writing.
  • Events outside our reasonable control, such as power or internet failure, or natural events.
