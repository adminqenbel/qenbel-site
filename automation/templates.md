# Canned replies

Turn these into Gmail Templates once: Gmail > Settings (gear) > See all settings > Advanced > Templates > Enable, then Save. Write a new email, paste a template, and choose More options (three dots) > Templates > Save draft as template > Save as new template. To use one later: compose or reply, then More options > Templates > pick it. Read it, fill in the `[brackets]`, and send.

Rules for every reply: no prices, no estimates, no delivery dates, no guarantees, no calls or meetings. Nothing is agreed until it is confirmed in writing by email.

---

## 1. What an Application Health Check involves

Hello [name],

Thank you for your interest. An Application Health Check is where we usually start. We review your existing application, including its code quality, architecture, security, dependencies and how it is set up to run, and we tell you plainly what matters most.

You receive a written report with what we found, what is most important, and a suggested order for fixing it.

To tell you how it would apply to your application, it would help to know:
- what the application does and who uses it
- how it was built (AI coding tools, developers, or both)
- what worries you most
- whether you have a launch or other deadline in mind

Please do not send passwords, API keys, customer data or source code in your reply. If we need anything like that, we will first agree a safe way to share it.

QenBel team

## 2. How we work

Hello [name],

This is how we normally work with a client:
1. You email us a short description of the application and what you need.
2. We reply by email with questions and what we would suggest.
3. We prepare a written proposal with scope, priorities, timeline, cost and deliverables.
4. When you approve it in writing, we start, usually with an Application Health Check.
5. We fix what matters most and send written updates by email.

We work only with your written authorisation, in isolated environments with limited access. We never change a production system without a verified backup, testing and your approval to release.

QenBel team
