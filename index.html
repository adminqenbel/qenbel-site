What we take on

Seven kinds of work. Most projects combine two or three.

  • Manage your software

    We run it day to day: updates, monitoring, fixes and incident response, so your team can keep building instead of keeping the lights on.

  • MCP servers

    We build and host MCP servers that let AI assistants use your data and tools, with the access limits you set.

  • Modernize your current software

    Keep what works and replace what doesn't. Upgrade frameworks, structure and performance in steps, without pausing the business.

  • Legacy to modern

    Move old desktop apps, on-premise servers and out-of-support stacks to current languages and the cloud, with your data intact.

  • AI bots inside your software, built secure

    Add assistants and automation to your product with permissions, data privacy and abuse protection designed in from the start.

  • Large refactors and system redesign with AI tools

    Untangle big codebases and redraw whole systems faster with AI-assisted analysis. A person reviews every change before it ships.

  • Front-end design, small to large

    From a single landing page to a full product interface: designed, built and tested on phones and desktops.

Everything happens by email

No portal to learn and no forms to fill in.

  1. You write to us

    Describe what you run, what hurts and when it needs to be fixed. Rough notes are fine.

  2. We reply with a plan

    You get questions, a written scope and a price, so you know what you are agreeing to.

  3. We do the work

    Progress, decisions and hand-over notes arrive in the same thread, where you can find them later.
