How we work

Everything happens by email, so there is a written record of every decision. No portal to learn.

  1. Enquiry

    You tell us about the application, your goal and the problems you see today.

  2. Technical discovery

    We review the stack, code, architecture and the access you approve.

  3. Assessment

    We find the quality, security, performance and reliability issues.

  4. Proposal

    You get the scope, priorities, timeline, cost and deliverables in writing.

  5. Implementation

    Code improvements, security fixes, testing and infrastructure work.

  6. Validation

    We verify function, quality, performance, security controls and readiness.

  7. Release

    Deployed with a verified backup, rollback arrangements and monitoring.

  8. Support

    Maintenance, updates, monitoring and continued improvement.

Our safety rule. We work only with your written authorisation, in isolated environments with least-privilege access. We never change your production system without a verified backup, testing and your release approval. Read how we handle compliance.
