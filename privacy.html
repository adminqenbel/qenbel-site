Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026. Last updated 5 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal data QenBel handles when you use qenbel.site, talk to our assistant, use the client portal at portal.qenbel.site, or email us, why we handle it, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and how you can control it. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it, and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

2. What we collect

We collect as little as we can. This website has no user accounts, no sign-in, no online payments and no advertising or analytics trackers. The client portal is separate and does have accounts: see section 14.

  • Visiting the website. Our hosting provider receives technical details every web server receives, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. We do not use these to identify you.
  • Using the assistant. The text you type into the assistant, and the assistant's replies, are sent to our server so a reply can be produced. See section 4.
  • Emailing us. Your email address, name if you give it, and whatever you write or attach. If you use the "Email the team" buttons in the assistant, your own email app or webmail opens with a draft that you review and send yourself. When your email reaches us, an automatic reply may confirm that it arrived.
  • Using the client portal. Your email address, your name and the details you give when you sign up, and what you and the QenBel team put into requests, messages and documents there. See section 14.
  • Working with us. If you become a client, we handle the business contact details and project information you give us, under a written agreement (see section 13).

Please do not send passwords, API keys, access tokens, payment card or bank details, government ID numbers or other customers' personal data in the assistant or in a first email. We will agree a safe way to share anything of that kind before any work starts.

3. Why we use it

  • To answer your questions and reply to your enquiries.
  • To assess whether and how we can help, and to prepare a proposal.
  • To deliver services under a signed agreement, send invoices and keep the records the law requires.
  • To run the client portal: to sign you in, decide who may see what, and let clients and the QenBel team follow requests, talk about them and share documents.
  • To keep the website and assistant secure and available, and to prevent abuse (for example limiting how many messages one address can send).
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We rely on your consent, which you give by choosing to use the assistant or to email us, and on the other lawful grounds the Digital Personal Data Protection Act allows, such as performing a contract you have asked for or complying with the law. You can withdraw consent at any time (section 10). We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or to build profiles.

4. The assistant

The assistant is an AI chatbot. When you send a message:

  • Your recent messages in that chat are sent from your browser to QenBel's server, which runs on a cloud hosting provider.
  • The server forwards them to Google's Gemini API, which writes the reply. Google processes the text to produce that reply.
  • QenBel's server does not store your conversation, and does not write message text to its logs. It keeps only short-lived counters (such as how many requests came from one address in the last minute) in memory to prevent abuse. These counters disappear when the server restarts.
  • Your chats are saved in your own browser so you can come back to them. They are not saved on our servers. You can delete them with the "Delete all chats on this device" button in the assistant's side menu, or by clearing site data in your browser.
  • The assistant is automated. It can be wrong, it does not give prices or legal advice, and it does not make decisions about you. The QenBel team confirms anything that matters by email.

Hosting and AI providers may keep their own technical logs under their own terms. We choose providers carefully, but we do not control their systems, which is another reason not to put sensitive information into the chat.

5. Cookies and browser storage

qenbel.site does not set cookies. (The client portal does: see section 14.) It uses your browser's local storage for two things only, and neither is sent to us:

  • a note of the day you last saw the opening animation, so it shows once a day;
  • your saved assistant chats on that device (section 4).

Our pages use one font, served from our own website. On qenbel.site we do not load fonts, scripts or trackers from other companies' servers, so no third party learns that you visited just because a page loaded. The portal is different: section 14 says which outside scripts it loads, and where.

6. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the website, the assistant and our email, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Website hosting: Vercel Inc., and Cloudflare, which manages our domain name and sits in front of the website.
  • Assistant server hosting: Render Services, Inc., and the network provider in front of it.
  • AI replies: Google (Gemini API).
  • Client portal: Supabase (the portal's database and private file storage), Render Services, Inc. (the portal's application), Clerk (sign-in), and Cloudflare (our domain, and visit counts on the portal's public pages). See section 14.
  • Email: our addresses at qenbel.site (such as contact@qenbel.site) are forwarded by ImprovMX to a Google (Gmail) mailbox, and our replies are sent through Gmail and SMTP2GO. A script in that mailbox sorts incoming mail and sends automatic acknowledgements. These providers handle your message and email address as it passes through them. Your own email service handles it too.

We may also disclose personal data where the law requires it, for example to a court, to a government authority, or to CERT-In, or to protect people from harm. We require our providers to protect the data they handle.

7. Transfers outside India

Several of our providers operate servers outside India, so your data may be processed in other countries when you use the website or the assistant. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act permits this except to countries the Government of India restricts, and we do not knowingly use providers in a restricted country. If you do not want your data processed abroad, please do not use the assistant and email us instead, and tell us in your email.

8. How long we keep it

  • Assistant chats: not kept by us. They stay in your browser until you delete them.
  • Enquiry emails that do not lead to work: deleted within 12 months of the last message.
  • Client records: kept for the life of the engagement and for as long afterwards as the law requires us to keep invoices and contracts, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Client portal information: how long it is kept has not been finalised. Until it is, it is kept while the account is in use and until QenBel deletes it or you ask us to (section 10).
  • Client source code, credentials and project data: handled under the written agreement, access removed and copies deleted when the work ends, unless the agreement or the law says otherwise.

We may keep data longer where needed to resolve a dispute, to enforce an agreement, or to meet a legal duty.

9. How we protect it

We use reasonable security safeguards, which include:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) for the website and the assistant's server;
  • an assistant server that stores no conversations and holds the AI key only in the host's protected settings, never in the website or the source code;
  • limits on how often one address can use the assistant, and checks that stop the assistant's output carrying links or addresses it should not;
  • least-privilege access, strong authentication for the accounts we use, and keeping client material out of unapproved tools;
  • for the client portal: sign-in handled by a specialist provider so that we hold no passwords, a permission check on the server for every page and download, each organisation's information kept apart from every other's, and files held privately and shared only through links that expire within about a minute.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee it. We describe our wider approach on the Compliance and Regulation page.

10. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us to:

  • give you a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it, and who we have shared it with;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it, unless we must keep it by law;
  • stop using it by withdrawing your consent (this does not affect what we did before you withdrew);
  • address a grievance (section 15);
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.

Email contact@qenbel.site with the subject "Privacy request". We may need to confirm that the request really comes from you before we act on it. We aim to reply within 7 days and will always reply within the time the law allows. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operating.

11. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

12. If something goes wrong

If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires, and we report cyber security incidents to CERT-In within six hours of noticing them, as the CERT-In Directions of 2022 require. We will tell you what happened, what data was involved and what you can do.

13. Client projects

Before we touch a client's code, systems or data, we agree the scope, access, confidentiality and security terms in writing. Where a project involves personal data that belongs to the client's own customers or staff, the client decides why and how it is used and QenBel processes it only on the client's written instructions, under a data processing agreement. We do not put client source code or personal data into third-party AI tools unless the client has permitted it in writing.

14. The client portal

The client portal at portal.qenbel.site is a private area for QenBel clients. It is an early version. The Portal notice inside the portal says the same in shorter form.

  • Who has an account. Anyone can create an account with an email address that they confirm with a code. Until QenBel approves it, the account can see only the list of our services and a form to tell us who you are and what you need. After approval it belongs to an organisation and, if a client, can see that organisation's requests, messages and documents.
  • What it keeps. Your email address; your name, company or team and the note you send when you ask for access; each request's title, description, scope, status and a record of every change, with who made it and when; the messages you and the QenBel team write on a request; the documents QenBel shares with you; and a log of actions (sign-ups, approvals, status changes and downloads) with the time and the address of the device they came from.
  • Sign-in. Clerk provides sign-in and sends the code to your email address. QenBel does not receive or store a password. Clerk sets the cookies that keep you signed in. Without them you cannot use the portal.
  • Where it is kept. The database and files are with Supabase. The application runs on Render. Servers may be outside India (section 7).
  • Outside scripts. After you sign in, the portal loads one script from other companies' servers: Clerk's, to keep your session fresh. The sign-in, sign-up, Portal notice and Contact pages also count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, and the sign-in and sign-up pages may also load Clerk's sign-in form and bot protection. Pages that show requests, messages or documents load nothing from Cloudflare.
  • Who can see it. People in your organisation can see their organisation's requests, messages and documents, and nobody else's. QenBel's team can see everything in the portal. Someone who has signed up but is not yet approved sees only the services list and their own request to join.
  • Please do not put in the portal passwords, keys, tokens or other secrets, or personal information about your own customers. Describe the work, not the credentials.
  • Keeping and deleting. See section 8. To see what the portal holds about you, correct it, or have it deleted, use the process in section 10.

15. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or complaints about personal data, contact our grievance contact, Hemapriyan R K, at contact@qenbel.site. We reply by email.

16. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices or the law change, and show the new date at the top. India's data protection rules are being brought into force in stages, so expect updates. If a change matters to you materially, we will say so on the website.
