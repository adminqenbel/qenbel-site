About QenBel

QenBel is a small software engineering company. We help startups, small businesses and growing teams make software they already have, built with AI coding tools, with developers, or both, secure, reliable and ready for real users.

How we work

We write down what we find, tell you plainly what matters, and work with you by email so every decision is on record. We start with an Application Health Check, then fix what matters most: code quality, security, performance and the steps needed to launch safely.

We work only with your written authorisation, and never change a production system without a verified backup, testing and your release approval. Read more on the Compliance and Regulation page.
